How iRadar works

iRadar watches {{ page_total }} Infineon pages and tells you what actually changed on them — text, documents, images, videos, boards, prices. It exists so nobody has to open a page and try to remember what it said last month.

The weekly check

Every {{ sched }}, automatically. Nobody starts it.

  1. Every page is opened in a real browser Not fetched as HTML — actually rendered, scrolled to the bottom to trigger anything that loads late, with tabs and accordions expanded. A page that only half-loads is thrown away rather than saved, because a half-page becomes next week's baseline and invents changes.
  2. What's on it is written down The visible text, every image, the full library (documents, videos, boards, design resources, partners), the price, and a screenshot.
  3. It's compared with last time Four independent signals, below. Any one of them is enough to flag.
  4. Anything flagged is captured a second time Immediately, before the run ends. If the difference doesn't reappear it was the website being inconsistent, not an edit, and it's dropped. This is what stops most false alarms.
  5. Failures get two more tries A page that times out is retried at the end of the run. Only something that fails every attempt is recorded as an error — a bad moment is not a verdict.
  6. What survives lands in your queue On the Dashboard, as Updated.

What counts as a change

SignalFlags when
TextWording is added, removed or edited. Text that merely moved does not count — the same lines in a new order is a layout change, not an edit.
LibraryA document, video, board, design resource or partner is added or removed, or one of their details changes (version, date, size, price).
ImagesA picture appears or disappears from the page.
ScreenshotMore than 1.5% of the pixels differ — a real visual change, not a shifted word.
File updatesA datasheet is republished at the same address. The page looks identical, the file behind it is not. Shown separately under File updates.

What it deliberately ignores

Each of these was a real flood of false alarms before it was fixed. If you ever wonder why something didn't flag, it is probably here.

IgnoredWhy
Stock levels"4387 in stock" becomes 4382 overnight. The price is still watched — a real price move flags.
The community forum feedRe-sorts and re-dates itself constantly. Whether the widget is there, empty, or has discussions is still tracked.
"Latest news" and the mega-menuIdentical on every page. One retired thumbnail once flagged 585 pages at once.
Tabbed sections loading differentlyTwo visits can land on different tabs. Their contents are tracked item by item instead, which doesn't flap.
Timestamps and "3 days ago"They change by themselves.

Reviewing an update

Open one from the Dashboard. You'll see exactly what moved, with the before and after. Then one of two buttons:

ButtonMeansUse it when
Accept This is the page now. It becomes the baseline the next check compares against. The change is real — whether it's important or not.
Reject Don't use this capture as the baseline. The previous version stays as the reference. The capture is wrong — something didn't load properly.

Accept is the normal one. Reject is for when iRadar got the page wrong, not for when you dislike the change. Either way the page goes back to normal afterwards; nothing is left hanging.

Notes

Every page, document, board and update has a Notes section at the bottom. Use it for the reasoning — "checked with marketing, the new subheadline is intentional". Whoever opens that page next reads it without having to ask you.

Edit or delete your own, mark any note done when it's handled, and see everything in one place under Notes.

The rest of the site

Dashboard Every tracked page, its state, and the review queue. Filter by type, status, or what content a page has.
Content Every document, video, image and board across all pages, and which pages each one appears on. The place to answer "where is this datasheet used?"
Hierarchy The whole site as a tree, built from the site's own structure.
Export Any of it as a spreadsheet.
Run history Every check, when it ran and what it found.

Accounts

Sign in with your Infineon email. Change your display name and password under the avatar, top right.

Admins manage people, settings, the page list and checks. Editors review updates and edit page details. Everyone can write notes.

Please use a passphrase — three or four random words like harbour-cactus-lantern-42. Long beats complicated.

Known limits

  • New pages aren't found automatically. iRadar watches the pages it's been given. If Infineon publishes a new one, someone has to add it under Add pages.
  • Five finder pages only capture their first page of results.
  • Gated documents (behind a MyInfineon login) can't be previewed — we only see the login wall.
