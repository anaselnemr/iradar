01The PTP / EB pill

Most questions are about part pages (PTPs) or evaluation boards (EBs). The pill is how you tell the Dashboard which one you mean.

  1. Open PTP / EB

    The lock on the button means nothing is picked yet, so all you see is the pill, with how many of each there are.

  2. Click PTPs or EBs

    The list narrows to those pages, and the menu opens up with what only they have: Perfect page, Product status and Buy button.

  3. Flip or let go

    Click the other side to switch. Your other filters stay put. Click the lit side again to go back to every page.

Picking PTP or EB under Type does the same thing.

02Finding pages

Tick and the list follows. There's no Apply button. Every filter you've set shows as a chip above the list, and its × removes it.

  • SearchName, title or address. SLE 78CFX1M10PH finds SLE-78CFX1M10PH.
  • TypePage type and level.
  • OwnershipBusiness line, product line, segment.
  • StatusUpdated, awaiting review, offline, paused… and the Archive.
  • Content & componentsWhat is on the page: documents, videos, images, key features, design resources tabs and the rest, each as Has or No.
  • ViewsSave a set of filters under a name. The whole team sees it.

Many pages at once: tick them (or Select all for everything that matches), then Capture, Approve updates, Pause, Archive, Export or Remove.

04Charts

Explore → Charts shows how complete PTPs and EBs are, by business line, product line and component. Live numbers.

  • Overview, Heatmap, Drill-down: three views of the same numbers.
  • Filters: the components you measure, which lines, online or offline, product status and buy button. SURF narrows to its lines and adds PCB design data.
  • PNG / SVG on every chart. Click a title to retype it for a slide first.

05How a check works

{#- NOT |capitalize: it lowercases the rest ("every monday") -#}

Runs {{ every }} at {{ at_time }} on its own{% if run_hours %}, and takes about {{ run_hours }}{% endif %}. Each page is opened in a real browser, read, and compared with last time.

{{ dia.flow() }} {{ dia.vote() }}

Anything that looks different is captured a second time. Only if both captures agree on what changed does it reach your queue, so a page that loaded badly doesn’t bother you. Pages that fail get two more tries.

The bar while it runs

  • No update, almost everything.
  • Updated, confirmed and in your queue.
  • Reconfirming, waiting for the second look. Not an update yet. It empties at the end, mostly into no update.
  • Blocked, will retry, retried at the end.
  • Waiting, not reached yet.

If the server restarts mid-check, it carries on where it stopped. To run one now, press Check all pages, or tick pages and choose Capture.

06What counts as a change

Six signals raise a page. The things on the right are ignored on purpose. Each one used to cause false alarms.

✓ Raises a page

  • TextWords added, removed or edited, including price and product status.
  • LibraryA document, video, board or partner comes or goes.
  • ImagesA picture comes or goes.
  • ScreenshotOver {{ s.visual_threshold }}% of pixels, or {{ s.height_threshold }}px of height.
  • Products tableIts parts count or columns change.
  • File updatesA new file behind the same link.

✕ Ignored on purpose

  • Stock levelsThey move every hour.
  • News, the menu, the community feedSame on every page, or re-sorted constantly.
  • “3 days ago”Changes with nobody editing.
  • Same content, new order or spacingLayout, not an edit.
  • Same picture, new sizeOne photo, several addresses.

07Reviewing an update

Open one from the Dashboard. It tells you what moved, then shows it: the text before and after, items in and out, and the changed area side by side, outlined in amber.

Approve

The new version is right. Next check compares against it.

The normal choice, whether the change matters or not.

Reject

The old version is right. It stays the reference.

When the capture is broken, or the change is a mistake that will be fixed. It keeps coming up until it is.

Each decision records who made it and when.

08Who owns a page

  • Parts and boards fill in themselves. PL from PIM, BL from the ES chart. The small PIM tag says so.
  • Set one by hand and it stays. No check overwrites it.
  • No source? It’s flagged, never guessed. Look for Ownership check on the Dashboard.
  • Application pages also have a segment (C3, A, I2).

09Content & files

Content lists every document, video, image and board once, with every page that uses it. That’s the answer to “if I change this datasheet, what am I touching?”

File updates is a second queue for files replaced behind the same link. The page looks identical, but the PDF is new. It has its own Approve and Reject.

10A page’s own page

Click any row for everything about one page.

Below that you’ll find the full library, every update it has ever had, and a notes box. Leave a note for whoever comes next: “checked with marketing, the new subheadline is intentional”. Every note lands in Notes too.

11Offline, paused, archived

  • Recently offlineThe site says 404. Still checked, in case it comes back.
  • PausedSkipped on purpose, but still counted.
  • ArchiveGone for good. Out of every count, chart and export. Restore brings one back.

To clear out dead pages: Status → Recently offline, Select all, Archive.

12Export

Explore → Export. One button gives you everything, or build your own table: pick the rows, pick the columns.

Useful columns: Perfect page, Missing key components (the to-do per page) and who owns it.

13Emails

Only if you switch on Email alerts in your profile.

  • A report after every scheduled check.
  • A Friday 08:00 reminder (German time), only if updates are still waiting.

That’s all it ever sends.

15Good to know

  • It only watches the pages it has. New pages on infineon.com need adding{% if is_admin %} with Add pages{% endif %}.
  • It keeps the last {{ s.keep_snapshots }} captures, not a full history.
  • It checks on a schedule, so a change made and undone in between is never seen.
  • Files behind a MyInfineon login can’t be previewed.
  • Admins can do everything. Editors review and edit pages. Everyone can leave notes.
  • Something broken or confusing? Say so at the top of Notes.
